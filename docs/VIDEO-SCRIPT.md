# Video script (under 3 minutes)

Record after PayPal sandbox and Gemini are connected, so both badges are green. Avoid third-party logos and copyrighted music.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A dispute email, then a countdown | Meet Maya. She's fictional, but seller forums are full of her story. She makes dinner sets by hand. This morning, a $186 PayPal dispute arrived. She has days to respond, and nobody to ask. |
| 0:15 | Forum patterns as plain text | Sellers keep saying the same thing: they don't know what wins, or why they lost. |
| 0:25 | Title card | Objection! lets small sellers rehearse a dispute before they answer it. Built with PayPal and Gemini. |
| 0:32 | Case list | It reads her open disputes straight from PayPal. |
| 0:40 | Evidence locker | She sees what evidence this dispute type needs, and what's missing. |
| 0:52 | Press Start the hearing | Now the part no form can do. One AI argues for the buyer as hard as the facts allow. Another defends Maya. A judge tests them both. |
| 1:15 | Verdict and weakest point | She learns her weak spot before PayPal does. |
| 1:25 | Cost comparison | The money isn't decided by AI. Our rules compare each option's expected cost, including dispute fees. |
| 1:40 | Review note, press File | Maya reviews the note. Nothing goes to PayPal until she says so. |
| 1:50 | PayPal activity log | That's a real sandbox call to PayPal's Disputes API. |
| 2:00 | Status shows With PayPal | Her evidence is in, and the case is now with PayPal for review. |
| 2:10 | Open the jacket case | Here's what makes it honest. On this $48 jacket, the evidence is thin. |
| 2:20 | Partial refund recommended | So it tells her not to fight. A partial refund is the cheapest path. |
| 2:30 | Tick photo evidence, strength rises | Each piece of evidence changes the math, in front of her. |
| 2:40 | Closing card | Big merchants have dispute teams. Now a seller with one kitchen table has a rehearsal room. Objection! |

Replace "sandbox call" with "simulated call" if it was recorded in simulated mode, or re-record.

Each sandbox dispute can only be filed once, so make fresh ones before recording. In the sandbox, the test ruling buttons only appear if PayPal allows a ruling. Don't claim a win on screen unless it really happened.

## Hosted demo

The Reset demo button restores the fictional cases, so judges can replay them. The hearing is limited to 30 runs an hour by default (`HEARINGS_PER_HOUR`), to protect your API credit.
