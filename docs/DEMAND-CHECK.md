# Seller demand check

Checked 5 November 2026.

## Verdict

Go, with one correction to the product. The pain is real and recurring, but "AI writes a better appeal" is the wrong pitch. Sellers' real problem is not knowing which fights can be won. The product must say when to stop, as well as how to fight.

## Rules worth building on

- A seller generally has about 10 days to respond to a claim, or it can close for the buyer.
- PayPal's help pages describe a Standard Dispute Fee, returned if a seller appeals and wins, and not charged on cases settled without escalation. This is the core of our cost comparison. One of those pages is older, so confirm current terms.
- High-volume sellers can face a 3-day response window and a higher fee.

## Limits of this check

- I read search excerpts, not full threads. PayPal Community, TalkBass, eBay, Discogs and PayPal help pages did not load for direct reading.
- Most threads are years old, so they show a lasting pattern, not current policy.
- I found no independent figure for how many sellers this affects. Do not put a market-size number in the pitch.
- No Reddit threads came back from search.
- No seller has been interviewed. One real conversation with a seller would be stronger than all of this.
